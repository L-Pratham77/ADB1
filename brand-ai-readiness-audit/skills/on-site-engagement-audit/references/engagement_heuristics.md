# On-Site Engagement & AI Visitor Orientation Heuristics

This reference details the behavioral dynamics of visitors arriving at a website via AI assistant citations (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Copilot) and provides the core heuristics for maximizing visitor engagement and retention.

---

## 1. The AI-Referred Visitor Journey

Visitors referred by AI search engines differ fundamentally from traditional organic searchers:

1. **Pre-Framed Context**: The visitor already read an AI summary that answered part of their question. They arrive seeking **verification**, **deeper technical proof**, **pricing details**, or a **direct trial/action**.
2. **Extreme Impatience (The 3-Second Rule)**: If the landing page does not immediately confirm the specific topic or product referenced by the AI assistant within 3 seconds, the visitor hits the back button (bounces).
3. **High Bounce Sensitivity**: A bounce signals to search algorithms and RAG feedback loops that the cited source failed to satisfy user intent.

---

## 2. Core Engagement Heuristics

### Heuristic 1: Immediate Above-the-Fold Orientation (The 3-Second Rule)
- The primary `<h1>` and supporting subhead must answer three questions without scrolling:
  1. *What is this product/service?*
  2. *Who is it built for?*
  3. *What is the specific primary outcome or benefit?*

### Heuristic 2: Action-Oriented CTA Hierarchy
- Every high-converting page requires an unambiguous primary Call to Action (CTA):
  - **Weak / High Friction**: *"Submit"*, *"Click Here"*, *"Learn More"*.
  - **Strong / Low Friction**: *"Start Free 14-Day Trial"*, *"View Live Demo"*, *"Read Documentation"*.
- Secondary CTA for lower-intent visitors (e.g., *"Explore GitHub"* or *"Watch 2-Min Overview"*).

### Heuristic 3: Scannability & Visual Chunking (The F-Pattern)
- Human readers scan web pages in an F-pattern.
- Walls of text (> 80 consecutive words without bullet points, bold headers, or callout boxes) induce cognitive fatigue.
- Best practice: Limit paragraphs to 2–3 sentences; utilize lists, comparison tables, and icon callouts.

### Heuristic 4: Context Retention Across the Journey
- Navigation breadcrumbs and clear section anchors orient the visitor within the broader site hierarchy.
- When arriving deep in documentation or a blog post, visible breadcrumbs enable instant navigation to product and pricing pages.
